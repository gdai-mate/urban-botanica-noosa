About Jan

A florist with a horticulturalist's eye

Jan Haebich Struthers is a qualified horticulturalist and landscape designer with training in fine art, and she has been designing and installing floral arrangements for over 25 years, from Port Douglas to Noosa.

Jan and her team have catered for resorts, weddings, conferences, corporate events, private functions and retail displays across the Sunshine Coast. She has a love for tropical flowers and foliage, and has put together just about every arrangement conceivable.
